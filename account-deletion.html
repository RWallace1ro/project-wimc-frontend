Delete Your Account or Data — What's In My Closet (WIMC)

Delete your entire account and all associated data

  1. Open the WIMC app and log in to your account.
  2. Tap the menu, then go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your password and confirm the deletion.

This permanently deletes your profile, all closet photos and videos, and all locally stored app data (Wish List, Shopping List, Donate Bin, Receipts, Saved Outfits, and preferences). This action cannot be undone.

Delete only some of your data (without deleting your account)

You can delete individual items at any time without deleting your whole account — clothing photos/videos, receipts, wish list items, shopping list items, and saved outfits can each be removed directly within the app from wherever they're displayed (e.g. tap an item, then Delete).

What is deleted, and what is retained

  • Your account profile (name, email) — deleted immediately.
  • Photos and videos stored via Cloudinary — deleted immediately.
  • App data stored in Firebase — deleted immediately.
  • Billing records: Stripe retains transaction records as required for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes even after account deletion, per standard payment-processing requirements.
